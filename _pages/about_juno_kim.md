**Juno Kim** is an Integrated M.S./Ph.D. student in the [Interdisciplinary Program in Artificial Intelligence](https://gsai.snu.ac.kr/en) at Seoul National University, advised by Prof. Byoung-Tak Zhang (Biointelligence Lab).

Research interests: Embodied AI, Vision-Language-Action (VLA) Models, 3D Scene Understanding, Spatial Reasoning, and Mobile Manipulation — with an emphasis on developing spatially grounded robot intelligence that integrates semantic and geometric understanding of indoor environments with generalizable robot policies, enabling mobile manipulators and humanoid robots to autonomously plan and execute complex, long-horizon tasks in real-world environments.
